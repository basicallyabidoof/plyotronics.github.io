About Us

Hardware nerds who ship.

Plyotronics is a small, passionate team of engineers and makers dedicated to bringing electronic ideas to life. Whether you need a one-of-a-kind prototype, a reliable sensor node, or a run of custom boards, we have the skills and tools to make it happen — on time and without the corporate overhead.

We believe great hardware is a blend of elegant design, solid firmware, and honest craftsmanship. Our workshop is stocked with oscilloscopes, soldering stations, 3-D printers, and enough dev boards to build a small city.

Services

What we do

End-to-end electronics development — from early concept sketches to finished, tested hardware ready for the real world.

🖥️
Design

PCB Design & Layout

Schematic capture, component selection, and multi-layer PCB layout using KiCad and Altium. Design-rule checked and ready for fab.

💾
Firmware

Embedded Firmware

Bare-metal C/C++, RTOS-based systems, and MicroPython — for ARM Cortex-M, ESP32, AVR, RISC-V, and more.

🔧
Assembly

Prototype Assembly

Hand-soldered SMD and through-hole assemblies, reflow oven runs, and functional bring-up testing for small batches.

📦
Enclosures

Enclosure & Mechanical

3-D printed and CNC-milled enclosures designed to fit your board — from utilitarian project boxes to polished consumer-grade housings.

🌐
IoT

IoT & Connectivity

Wi-Fi, Bluetooth LE, LoRaWAN, and cellular integrations paired with lightweight cloud dashboards and MQTT/REST backends.

🔍
Consulting

Technical Consulting

Design reviews, failure analysis, compliance guidance (FCC/CE), and second opinions before you commit to a production run.

Products

Off-the-shelf hardware

A growing line of open-source dev boards and sensor modules available for direct purchase — ready to drop into your next build.

🟢
Dev Board

PlyoCore v2

ESP32-S3 based development board with USB-C, LiPo charging, and a generous GPIO breakout. Open-source hardware — KiCad files on GitHub.

🌡️
Sensor Module

EnviroNode

Compact environmental sensor breakout combining temperature, humidity, pressure, and ambient light — I²C, 3.3 V, breadboard friendly.

Power Module

BatPack

Intelligent LiPo / Li-ion management module with USB-C PD input, 4-level LED gauge, and clean 3.3 V and 5 V rails for your projects.
